Get transcript on YouTube
  1. On the video page, click the description under the video (it says …more).
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the description and click Show transcript.
  3. In the transcript panel, drag to select all the text, then copy it.
  4. Come back here and paste it below. Timestamps are fine to leave in.

This step is easier on a computer than a phone. In Level 3 it becomes automatic.
